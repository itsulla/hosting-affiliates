Commercial relationships without editorial drift

An independent field guide for turning AI-generated application code into a reviewed, secure, maintainable deployment.

Plain-language disclosure

Some outbound links are marked sponsored. If you follow one and later qualify under the merchant's terms, the publisher may receive cash commission, account credit, or another referral reward. There is no added charge from using the marked link.

Confirmed direct referral links are published for DigitalOcean and Hostinger. Confirmed affiliate relationships include Railway directly, plus Bluehost and Namecheap through Impact. Liquid Web and Vultr are not approved and therefore receive no monetized CTA. Netlify remains editorial unless a relationship is confirmed.

Editorial independence: what does not change

  • Warnings, restrictions, and non-affiliate alternatives stay in the comparison.
  • A merchant cannot buy a ranking or suppress a material caveat.
  • Unapproved applications are not presented as active programs.
  • Provider-specific referral rewards are not relabeled as cash commissions.
  • Changing prices and terms must be checked at the destination before purchase.

How marked clicks are measured

The site records an affiliate_click event with contextual dimensions. The destination excludes query parameters so referral codes are not copied into analytics.

Decision rule: technical fit, security, reversibility, commercial-use terms, and total cost come before commission.
