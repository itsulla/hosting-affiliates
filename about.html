Independent by design

An independent field guide for turning AI-generated application code into a reviewed, secure, maintainable deployment.

The publication covers output inspection, hosting compatibility, deployment workflows, production failure modes, domains, and provider relationships. It does not treat an AI builder as proof that a generated application is secure or production-ready.

What readers should expect

  • Primary-source links beside volatile or consequential claims.
  • Explicit checked dates rather than undated “current” language.
  • Clear separation between facts, estimates, account status, and editorial judgment.
  • No paid ranking, sponsored placement, or claim that commission makes a product better.
